      Clues

      How to play

      Every day there's a new mystery country, shown only as a blank outline. You have 6 guesses.

      Each wrong guess tells you how far away and in which direction the mystery country is, and unlocks a new clue:

      1. Region & hemisphere
      2. The map zooms out to show the neighbours
      3. Size & coastline
      4. Languages, currency & the capital's first letter
      5. The flag

      A new country unlocks at midnight, your local time.
